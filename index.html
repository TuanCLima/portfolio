Leruna

Read beyond the page · native iOS app

An AI reading companion that reads stories aloud with low-latency voice, answers questions about the book you're in, explains passages and illustrates scenes.

  • Real-time spoken reading with Gemini Live / OpenAI Realtime. The iPhone connects directly to the audio model using short-lived tokens issued by the backend, so audio never round-trips through the server.
  • Convex backend for story metadata, chunked text and synced reading progress. Public-domain classics are processed once into shared canonical chunks.
  • Bounded-context Q&A: answers start from compact local matches, then the model requests chapter-aware sections until it can answer.
  • Multi-provider fallback chains for text and illustration models, with an atomic spend ledger capping image costs. Clerk handles auth (Apple, Google, magic link).

How I work

Most of my work sits at the seams between systems.

That's usually where the interesting problems are, and where a careful engineer saves a team the most time.

01

Hostile runtimes are the interesting ones

Office add-ins, video players, cross-platform apps: environments where you don't control the platform and APIs differ between versions. I like mapping the constraints and building an abstraction the rest of the team can work above.

02

Product sense, not just tickets

Scheduling a deposition is a legal workflow before it's a form. Watching a novela is a habit before it's a player. I learn what the user is actually doing, then cut the interface down to that.

03

Tested, observable, documented

Clean architecture, strong unit tests and monitoring shipped alongside the feature, because in an embedded add-in or a TV player you often can't reproduce the bug on your own machine.
